# Measurements

This chapter defines how each effect is measured. Thresholds for lowercase and uppercase letters are in [Effect Letters](../rating/effect-letters.md).

1. Every applicable measurement shall be taken at baseline and after each level.
2. An effect that does not apply to a unit (for example, closure gap on a slab phone) shall be recorded as N/A.

## Methods

| Effect (letter) | How to measure | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Closure gap (`c`) | Insert a feeler gauge gently, without forcing, at three points along the free edge. Record the largest | Hinged or closing devices |
| Hold force (`h`) | Tape a string loop to the centre of the free edge. Pull perpendicular with the luggage scale or gauge until it opens. Record the average of the peak readings of 3 pulls | Devices with magnets, detents or latches |
| Rough motion (`r`) | Record audio and video of 10 cycles from 30 cm, in a quiet room. Note anything heard or felt | Devices with moving parts |
| Buttons and ports (`b`) | Press each button or key 5 times and check that every press registers. Insert a plug into each port | All |
| Screen (`s`) | Display full-screen white, mid-grey and black. Photograph at 0° and 45° under the same lighting. Draw a grid in a drawing app to check touch | Devices with displays |
| Ingress (`i`) | Inspect seams, gaps, grilles and ports with a flashlight (UV light if a tracer was used) | All |
| Enclosure breach (`E`) | Lab teardown (see [Test Tiers](tiers.md#lab-tier-teardown)); electrical faults observed during use | D, G, W |
| Fogging (`f`) | [TBD] | W |
| Audio (`m`) | [TBD], after the drying period [TBD] | W |
| Wear condition | [TBD] (see [U: Usage](../groups/usage.md)) | Lab units |
