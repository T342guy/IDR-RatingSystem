# Measurements

This chapter defines how each effect is measured. Thresholds for lowercase and uppercase letters are in [Effect Letters](../rating/effect-letters.md).

1. Every applicable measurement shall be taken at each group's [baseline](conditions.md#baselines) and after each level or step.
2. An effect that does not apply to a unit (for example, closure gap on a slab phone) shall be recorded as N/A.
3. Sound-level readings shall use the same app, the same second phone, the same distance and the same position at every measurement of a unit.

## Methods

| Effect (letter) | How to measure | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Closure gap (`c`) | Insert a feeler gauge gently, without forcing, at three points along the free edge. Record the largest | Hinged or closing devices |
| Hold force (`h`) | Tape a string loop to the centre of the free edge. Pull perpendicular with the luggage scale or gauge until it opens. Record the average of the peak readings of 3 pulls | Devices with magnets, detents or latches |
| Rough motion (`r`) | See [Rough motion](#rough-motion) | Devices with moving parts |
| Buttons and ports (`b`) | Press each button or key 5 times and check that every press registers. Insert a plug into each port | All |
| Screen (`s`) | Display full-screen white, mid-grey and black. Photograph at 0° and 45° under the same lighting. Draw a grid in a drawing app to check touch | Devices with displays |
| Casing damage (`k`) | Inspect every face, edge and camera lens cover under bright light at 0° and 45°. Photograph any damage, with a ruler in frame | All |
| Ingress (`i`) | Inspect inside ports, grilles, holes and gaps with a flashlight (UV light if a tracer was used). Record only medium or water below the outer surface | All |
| Enclosure breach or electrical fault (`E`) | Watch for electrical faults during exposure and at each measurement. Breaches: lab teardown (see [Test Tiers](tiers.md#lab-tier-teardown)) | All |
| Fogging (`f`) | See [Fogging](#fogging) | Devices with cameras or displays, in W |
| Audio (`m`) | See [Audio](#audio) | Devices with speakers or microphones, in W |
| Wear condition | Simulated wear level, or [equivalent level](../groups/usage.md#equivalent-level-for-real-wear) for real wear | All units |

## Rough motion

1. In a quiet room, record the ambient sound level for 10 seconds with a sound-level meter app on the second phone, 30 cm from the unit.
2. Record audio and video of 10 cycles from the same position. Note anything felt.
3. Record the highest A-weighted level the app shows during the 10 cycles.
4. The baseline level is the level recorded at the group's baseline. If that is within 3 dB of the ambient level, the ambient level is used as the baseline instead.

## Fogging

1. Immediately after each W step, and again at the end of the drying period, inspect each camera lens and the display under a bright light at 45°, with the display showing full-screen white and then black.
2. Photograph each lens with the second phone.
3. At the end of the drying period, take a photo with each of the unit's cameras of a plain, evenly lit surface and compare it with the baseline photo for haze or spots.

## Audio

Measure at the end of the drying period. Record the ambient level first.

1. **Speakers:** play a 1 kHz tone at maximum volume from each speaker. Measure with a sound-level meter app on the second phone, 10 cm in front of the speaker opening. Record the A-weighted level averaged over 5 seconds.
2. **Microphones:** play a 1 kHz tone from the second phone at a fixed volume, 10 cm from each microphone opening. Record 5 seconds with a recorder app on the unit that displays input level, and record the peak level shown.
3. Listen to each speaker and recording for distortion, crackling or buzzing.

## Judgement calls

The `b`, `k` and `s` thresholds depend partly on the tester's judgement.

1. The baseline and per-level or per-step photos, audio and video for these effects shall be kept with the report, so others can review each call.
2. Each call should be confirmed by a second person where one is available. The report shall state whether it was.
