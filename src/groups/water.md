# W: Water

## What W tests

W tests resistance to fresh water at increasing depth and duration.

## Medium

W tests shall use fresh water only.

> **Note (informative):** Salt and chlorinated water are planned as future groups.

## Scale

W is graded on two scales.

| Depth tier | Depth |
|---|---|
| 1 | Spray / splash |
| 2 | 1 m |
| 3 | 2 m |
| 4 | 4 m |
| 5 | 6 m |

| Time tier | Duration |
|---|---|
| 1 | 10 min |
| 2 | 30 min |
| 3 | 1 h |
| 4 | 4 h |

## Grading

1. W shall be written `W{depth}{time}`.
2. The **depth digit** is the deepest depth tier passed at time tier 1.
3. The **time digit** is the longest time tier passed at that depth.
4. A unit that fails depth tier 1 shall be written `W0` with its failure letter and no time digit.
5. Effect letters shall be written per [Code Format](../rating/code-format.md#effect-letters). When a failure letter is written above `W0` is [TBD].

Example (hypothetical): `W52` means the unit passed 6 m (depth tier 5) for 30 minutes (time tier 2).

## Water-specific effects

| Letter | Effect |
|---|---|
| `f` | Fogging or condensation inside a lens or display |
