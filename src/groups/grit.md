# G: Grit

## What G tests

G tests **mechanism tolerance**: whether moving parts keep working with debris inside them.

**Threat:** coarse particles jamming or grinding moving parts.

## Medium

The G medium is defined in [Test Media](../equipment/media.md).

## Full dose

| Symbol | Value |
|---|---|
| F | **100 g** per 100 cm² of exposure area |

## Scale, procedure and grading

G uses the shared scale, dose formula, procedure and grading in [D and G: Particulate Testing](particulate.md).
